Our Story

Building Excellence Since 2015

HRS Engineers & Builders was established in 2015 by Rathish Kumar with a clear vision: to be the most trusted and results-driven construction company in Coimbatore. What began as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into a 50+ strong team of professionals delivering landmark projects across Tamil Nadu.

Over the past decade, we have completed over 500 projects spanning luxury villas, corporate offices, industrial facilities, educational institutions, and healthcare buildings. Every structure we build is a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and client satisfaction.
