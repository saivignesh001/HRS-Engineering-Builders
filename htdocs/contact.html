FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the construction cost per sqft in Coimbatore? +

Residential construction in Coimbatore typically ranges from ₹1,800–₹3,500 per sqft depending on specifications. Basic construction starts at ₹1,800/sqft, standard at ₹2,200–₹2,600/sqft, and premium/luxury at ₹3,000–₹3,500/sqft. Commercial projects range from ₹2,200–₹4,000/sqft. Contact us for an exact quote based on your specific requirements.

How long does residential construction take? +

A typical 2,000–3,000 sqft house takes 10–14 months. Larger villas and apartment complexes take 18–30 months. We always provide a detailed timeline in our project agreement and stick to it with rigorous project management.

Do you provide turnkey construction services? +

Yes! Our turnkey construction service covers everything from initial design to final handover — architecture, structural engineering, construction, electrical, plumbing, finishing, and interior work. You simply hand over the keys and collect back a complete, ready-to-use building.

Is there a warranty on construction work? +

Yes. We provide a 2-year structural warranty and 1-year warranty on all civil finishing work. Any defects that arise from our construction within the warranty period are rectified at no additional cost to the client.

Do you help with building plan approvals and permits? +

Yes. We assist with architectural drawings for Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation (CCMC) approval, Tamil Nadu building regulations compliance, and all necessary construction permits. Our team has extensive experience navigating local regulatory requirements.
