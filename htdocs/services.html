Industrial

Industrial Construction Services

We have extensive expertise in industrial construction — factories, manufacturing plants, warehouses, and distribution centers. Industrial structures require specialized engineering knowledge for heavy load capacities, crane systems, chemical resistance, and fire suppression.

Our industrial construction team works closely with your production engineers to design facilities that maximize operational efficiency and worker safety.

  • Pre-engineered steel structures (PEB)
  • Large-span warehouse construction
  • Heavy industrial floor design (up to 15T/sqm load)
  • Industrial drainage & effluent treatment systems
  • Overhead crane runway systems
  • Factory layouts for maximum operational flow

Turnkey

Turnkey Construction Solutions

Our turnkey construction service takes complete responsibility for your project from initial concept to final handover. As your single point of contact, we manage architecture, engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning — giving you peace of mind and a ready-to-use building.

Turnkey contracts eliminate coordination headaches between multiple contractors and significantly reduce project risk and cost overruns.

  • Single-point accountability for the entire project
  • Design, build, and furnish in one contract
  • Guaranteed fixed-price and timeline delivery
  • Complete documentation and approvals handled
  • Furnished and move-in ready handover
  • 2-year post-handover warranty
